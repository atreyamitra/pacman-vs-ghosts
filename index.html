🎲 Fuzzy-logic ghostsNEW

Soft Computing · Fuzzy Logic. Each ghost fuzzifies its distance to Pac-Man, Pac-Man's lives and the pellets left, fires 11 IF-THEN rules and defuzzifies them into an aggression from 0 to 1. Aggressive ghosts run up to 12% faster and skip their scatter phase; timid ones are up to 12% slower.

Switch it on and start a game to see live values.

🤖 Neural-network ghostNEW

Soft Computing · Neural Networks. A small MLP (17 → 10 tanh → 4 softmax) learns from backpropagation which way Pac-Man turns at junctions. Pinky then ambushes the predicted spot instead of "4 tiles ahead". It learns while you play and keeps what it learned in this browser.

How it works
  • A* expands the node with the lowest f = g + h, where g is steps walked and h is the Manhattan distance to the target. With an admissible h it always finds a shortest path while expanding far fewer nodes than Dijkstra.
  • Four personalities = four different targets fed into the same A*: Blinky targets Pac-Man, Pinky targets 4 tiles ahead, Inky mirrors Blinky around Pac-Man, Clyde chases until he gets close and then retreats to his corner.
  • Minimax looks depth rounds ahead (Pac-Man move, then each ghost move) and picks the move with the best worst-case evaluation. The evaluation rewards pellets, penalises nearby ghosts and pulls toward the closest food.
  • Alpha-Beta skips branches that cannot change the final choice. Same answer, far fewer nodes — use the experiment above to see the saving.
  • Power pellets frighten the ghosts, and eaten ghosts use A* to run back home. Each ghost can be eaten once per power pellet.
  • Fuzzy logic turns crisp numbers (distance, lives, pellets left) into degrees of "Near / Medium / Far" etc., fires IF-THEN rules (AND = min) and averages their outputs (Sugeno style) into one aggression value.
  • Neural network: a 17-10-4 MLP trained online by backpropagation (SGD, cross-entropy) predicts Pac-Man's turn at junctions; Pinky aims at the predicted tile.
  • Genetic Algorithm: chromosome = 4 Minimax weights; elitism (2), tournament selection (k = 3), arithmetic crossover, log-normal mutation; fitness = mean score over seeded games; champion validated on unseen games.
